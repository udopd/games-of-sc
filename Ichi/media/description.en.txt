Ichi and the Boxes is a cozy pixel-art Sokoban game starring a tidy Shiba Inu. Push boxes onto green pads, plan your moves, and solve 25 handcrafted-style puzzles. Enjoy smooth animations, a calm Lo-fi palette, and chunky pixel outlines. Play with a keyboard, mouse, or swipes; all levels are available immediately. Signed-in players on SUPER CUBE can sync their progress to the cloud.
